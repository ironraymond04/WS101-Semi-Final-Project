# Campus Lost and Found

This context helps one campus community report lost and found property, verify ownership, and return items held by the campus.

## Language

**Lost report**:
A student's open statement that they are looking for an item, describing when and where it was last seen.
_Avoid_: Lost item record, claim

**Found notice**:
A report that a finder or admin encountered an item, whether or not the item has reached the campus desk.
_Avoid_: Found item record

**Custody**:
The campus desk's responsibility for a found item after an admin records that it was received.
_Avoid_: Reported, found

**Possible match**:
A candidate relationship between a lost report and a found notice; it is not proof of ownership.
_Avoid_: Confirmed match

**Claim**:
A student's request to receive a found item that has been recorded in campus custody.
_Avoid_: Match, report

**Claim attempt**:
One preserved submission and review cycle for a student's claim; a rejected attempt may be followed by a new attempt.
_Avoid_: Claim (when referring to an individual retry)

**Reservation**:
The temporary allocation of a desk-held found item to a claimant after admin approval and before handover.
_Avoid_: Returned

**Handover**:
The physical transfer of a desk-held item to its verified owner, recorded by an admin.
_Avoid_: Approved, claimed
